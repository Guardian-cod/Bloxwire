Murder Mystery 2 came out in January 2014. Twelve years later it's one of the busiest games on Roblox again. Its Halloween 2026 event started on October 18, and the server count shot up. The tracker Bloxdatabase logged about 266,000 people playing at once on the morning of Oct 19 (UTC). By Oct 23 at 05:00 UTC, Secretblox showed more than 622,000, plus a 7-day change of "+500%". Allthings.how's event calendar put it at about 598,000 around Oct 22–23. Counts swing a lot through the day, but every tracker points the same way: lots of people are coming back to MM2.

The event runs until **November 16**. That gives you a little over three weeks. Here's what's new, what's worth chasing and how to avoid wasting Candy.

## Quick refresher: how MM2 works

If you've never played, or it's been years: each round, one player is secretly the **Murderer** and one is the **Sheriff**. Everyone else (up to ten players) is **Innocent**. The Murderer has to eliminate everyone. The Sheriff has the only gun that can stop them. Innocents run, hide and try to work out who's holding the knife. If the Sheriff goes down, an Innocent can pick up the dropped gun and become the hero.

Rounds are short, quick to understand and very easy to jump into with friends. That's a big part of why the game has lasted so long.

## What the Halloween event adds

Developer Nikilis went retro this year. Theclick.gg (Oct 19) reports that the event brings back the look of older MM2 Halloweens, including a smaller witch-themed lobby. A Haunted House-style map is back too, and older themed maps rejoin the rotation. If you played MM2 as a younger kid, this is the nostalgia event.

The main changes:

- **Candy replaces Coins.** Normal Coins don't drop during the event. You collect Candy on the map instead, and some pickups give more than one.
- **A 30-tier Halloween Battle Pass**, paid for with Candy.
- **The Halloween Box**, the only weapon crate during the event.
- **Halloween Bingo**, a 3×3 board of challenges with knife skins as rewards.
- **A Cauldron** in the lobby (behind the event NPC) for crafting the top item of the season.

## How to earn Candy fast

Candy pays for everything, so this is where your time goes.

1. **Do all three Daily Quests every day.** They refresh at 8 AM UTC, so check what that is in your time zone. Finishing all three gives a **500-Candy bonus**, according to TechWiser. Over the rest of the event that's roughly 12,000 Candy from the bonus alone, if you never miss a day.
2. **Grab Candy before chasing objectives.** As an Innocent, sweeping the map for Candy is usually worth more than hiding in a corner.
3. **Work through Bingo on the side.** Most squares happen while you play anyway: survive three rounds as an Innocent, collect 800 Candy, shoot the Murderer as Sheriff, and so on. Finishing columns gives Halloween Keys, and finishing the whole board gives the Cauldron item.

## The Battle Pass, tier by tier

The pass has 30 tiers. Here's how the costs add up, based on TechWiser and theclick.gg:

- **Tier 1** is free (a Zombie Plush).
- **Tiers 2–29** cost 600 Candy each, so 16,800 Candy in total. Along the way you unlock cosmetics, knife and gun skins and the Emberknife at tier 29.
- **Every odd tier from 3 to 27** gives a **Halloween Key**, 13 in all.
- **Tier 30** is the **Emberbow**, an Ancient gun, and it costs **100,000 Candy** on its own.

The full pass comes to about 116,800 Candy. Some listings say 117,400, so check the price in-game before you plan around it.

## The Halloween Box and the Cauldron

Each Halloween Box costs 600 Candy or one Key. The rarest pull is the Godly **Embersickle**, and there's an even rarer Chroma version. Both sources stress that the odds are luck-based. Nikilis hasn't published exact Chroma odds, and the drop table TechWiser lists doesn't even add up to 100%, so treat any odds you see online as guesses.

The season's top item is the **Emberscythe**, an Ancient knife. You craft it at the Cauldron by combining one Emberbow and one Embersickle, and there's a small chance of a Chroma version. That means you need the full Battle Pass *and* a lucky box pull.

Be realistic. Spend your Keys on boxes, because they're free. But don't burn Candy on boxes until you've cleared the pass tiers you want, or you could reach November 16 with neither.

## What about the paid stuff?

There's also a paid **Mortal Bundle** for 3,399 Robux. It includes the Mortal knife, the Immortal gun and a Smolder effect. The two weapons are also sold separately for 1,699 Robux each, but Smolder only comes with the bundle (prices from theclick.gg, Oct 19). None of it is needed to enjoy the event. Everything above can be earned just by playing.

One more thing: rare MM2 items always attract trade scams. Never give your account details to anyone, and ignore "free Godly" offers outside the game. If it sounds too good to be true, it's a scam.

## The verdict

Yes, it's worth playing. Even if you never touch the Emberbow, the Daily Quests, Bingo and the cheap pass tiers give you a pile of Halloween skins for a few rounds a day. The retro lobby and old maps are a nice throwback for anyone who played years ago, and with this many people online, servers fill up fast. Just pace yourself: the event runs until November 16, and the 100,000-Candy finale is for dedicated players, not something to stress about.
