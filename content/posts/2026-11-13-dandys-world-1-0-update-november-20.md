Dandy's World is finally growing up. BlushCrunch Studio announced on November 11 that its horror survival hit will leave Alpha with **version 1.0 on Friday, November 20, 2026**. The update adds the game's first brand-new game mode, a fresh route with more than ten new floors, and a card system nobody has seen in action yet. If you have been playing since the [ALPHA] tag went up more than two years ago, there is also a badge reward waiting for you.

Here is everything that has been confirmed so far, what is still a mystery, and what you should do before next Friday.

## When does Dandy's World 1.0 come out?

The date everyone agrees on is **Friday, November 20, 2026**. The exact time is a little less clear:

- Endgame Tools lists **20:00 UTC** (3 PM ET, noon PT, 9 PM in Berlin), based on Roblox's own event page, "Dandy's World 1.0 Release", which runs from November 20 to November 30.
- Gaming Route says BlushCrunch has not posted an official start time, and that the 19:00 UTC time floating around comes from a fan-run countdown, so treat it as unofficial.

Our advice: check the official Roblox event page on the day. If you want to be in a server the second it drops, being online a bit before 19:00 UTC covers both times.

## What's new in 1.0

Most of the feature details come from IGN's report on the announcement, which both Endgame Tools and Gamebastion relay. According to them, 1.0 brings:

- **A new game mode.** It is the first new mode the game has ever had, and it is described as a harder alternative to the normal survival run.
- **A new path through Gardenview Center** with more than ten new floors. IGN calls them "the deepest and darkest floors" in the game.
- **A card system.** How it works has not been shown yet.
- **A new coordinator character** whose choices change depending on how you approach each run.
- **More than 10,000 words of new lore and dialogue** about the Malfunction Incident, which is great news if you are into the story side of the game.

One honest caveat: as of November 11, nobody outside the studio had seen how the new mode or the cards actually play. Full patch notes had not been published yet either, according to Gaming Route. So expect surprises, good or bad.

## More announcements around launch

The 1.0 update is not coming alone. Endgame Tools reports a few extras:

- On **November 19**, the day before launch, the full theme song of the fictional *Dandy's World* animated series goes up on music platforms.
- An **animated music video** will introduce a brand-new character.
- The **Christmas event is coming back**, though no dates have been given yet.

Gamebastion also quotes BlushCrunch's president, Joe Jurbala, saying "this is just the beginning for Dandy's World". So 1.0 sounds more like a new starting line than a finish.

## Get the Alpha Player! badge before it's too late

This is the part you can act on right now. Dandy's World has a badge called **Alpha Player!**, which you earn by playing during the Alpha. Its description on Roblox says it "will give you a reward" when the game fully releases. What that reward is had not been revealed as of November 11.

The badge is not exactly rare: Endgame Tools counted it as awarded **113,995,906 times** on November 11. But if you have never played, or you are not sure whether you have it, jumping in before November 20 is the safest move, since the badge is tied to playing during the Alpha.

While you are there: the current version is 0.30.1, the "Astro quests and rework" update, and Gaming Route notes that the Astro Toon of the Week runs until **today, November 13, at 3 PM EST (20:00 UTC)**.

## How big is Dandy's World right now?

Big, but it has cooled off since its October peak. Some numbers, all from Endgame Tools' readings of Roblox's charts (not official Roblox data):

- **239,782 players** on October 10, its highest since September 30, when it was the No. 4 game on Roblox.
- **202,573 players** on November 7, still No. 6.
- **86,994 players** at 00:41 UTC on November 11, down to No. 11.
- **69,863 players** on November 12 at 19:27 UTC, No. 12 on the most-played list.

On our own [weekly chart](/#chart-title) it came back into the top 10 at No. 9 this week. The game also had **7,938,305,286 visits** when Endgame Tools checked on November 11, and Gamebastion counts more than 40 playable Toons.

That dip after Halloween is pretty normal. The real question is whether 1.0 brings players back to those 200,000-plus numbers. A new mode, new floors and a badge payout is exactly the kind of update that tends to do that, so do not be surprised if Dandy's World climbs the charts again on November 20.

## The verdict

If you already play Dandy's World, November 20 is a date to circle. A harder mode and more than ten new floors is the biggest content drop the game has announced in a long time, and the lore fans get a huge pile of new story.

If you have never tried it, this week is actually the best time to start: you can still pick up the Alpha Player! badge, learn the basics on the normal floors, and be ready when the hard stuff arrives. Just keep your expectations in check until the cards and the new mode are actually playable. We will know a lot more next Friday.
