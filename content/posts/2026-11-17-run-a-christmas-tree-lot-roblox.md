It's not even Thanksgiving, and Roblox already has a Christmas hit. **Run a Christmas Tree Lot** is a small business sim where you sell Christmas trees, and over the past week it has been one of the fastest-growing games on the whole platform. The tracker Rokoala lists it as the **No. 1 gainer over the last 7 days** (checked Nov 17), up about **907%**, from roughly 790 players to about 8,000. A weekend event pushed it much higher than that for a while.

So what is it, why is it suddenly everywhere, and should you bother? Let's go.

## How big did it get?

Here's the catch with this game: its player count moves in waves, so the number you see depends a lot on *when* you look. Here's what the trackers showed when we checked:

- **7-day growth (Rokoala, checked Nov 17):** about 792 players a week ago, about 8,000 now. That's the biggest weekly jump of any game on Rokoala's list.
- **24-hour growth (rblxdb, checked Nov 17):** from about **6,000 to about 32,600 players** in a little over 24 hours, a jump of around 420%. That put it at **No. 6** on rblxdb's list of the fastest-growing games that day.
- **Biggest peak so far:** BloxRadar recorded a daily high of about **72,000 players on Nov 15**. It has only tracked the game since Nov 12, so that is the highest number *it* has seen, not an all-time record.
- **Sunday evening (BloxRadar):** about **18,100 players** at 21:49 UTC on Nov 16.

And the dip: Rokoala's 24-hour list shows the game *down* about 73% at the same time, from about 29,100 to 8,000. The trackers didn't sample at the same moments, so they don't agree. What they all show is the same pattern: big spikes around events, then a drop back down.

For scale, that's still a small game next to the giants. Steal An Egg had about **514,000 players** on Endgametools on Nov 16. Run a Christmas Tree Lot isn't anywhere near our weekly chart. It's interesting because of how fast it grew, and because it's a Christmas game taking off in mid-November.

## How does it play?

The idea is simple, and guide sites describe it the same way. You run a Christmas tree lot. You order different types of trees, put them out for customers, and sell them for Cash. Then you spend that money to grow the business:

- **Stock up:** order trees and keep your lot full so customers always have something to buy.
- **Hire workers:** helpers keep the lot running so you don't have to do every sale yourself.
- **Upgrade and expand:** more space and better upgrades mean more trees and more money.
- **Reputation:** the game also has a Reputation stat that you earn alongside Cash.

If you've played any Roblox tycoon, you already know the loop: earn, upgrade, earn faster. The seasonal theme is what makes it stand out. Selling trees in a snowy lot feels cozy in a way most tycoons don't.

The developer is listed as **Mr. Grinchmas**, also going by **Mr GM Studio** (per BloxRadar, TechWiser and Roonby). The earliest guide we found, on GamerTweak, is from **Nov 7**, so the game only started getting attention in the last ten days or so.

## Why now? Events, mostly

The big spike lines up with a live event. BloxRadar lists **"Christmas Event Part 2"** for **Sunday, Nov 15 at 20:00 UTC**, with a Krampus storyline. That's the same day it recorded the 72,000-player peak. BloxRadar also says the game got another update on Nov 16.

That's a common pattern on Roblox: a scheduled event pulls in a crowd, the crowd tells friends, and the game climbs the "fastest-growing" lists. Then most of the visitors leave until the next event. Whether this game keeps a steady audience will depend on whether more parts of the Christmas story are coming. We couldn't find an official schedule for future events.

It also helps that there's very little Christmas competition this early. The big games mostly haven't started their winter events yet. Adopt Me!, for example, opened its Christmas events on Nov 30 in 2023, Dec 6 in 2024 and Dec 5 in 2025, according to a roundup on BloxUltra that cites the official patch notes. As of that article's last update on Oct 26, nothing had been confirmed for 2026. So for now, a small game has the Christmas mood almost to itself.

## What about codes?

Guide sites list a bunch of codes for free Cash and Reputation, but they don't agree with each other. TechWiser (updated Nov 16) lists several codes that no other site has, and says two older ones have expired, while other guides still call those same codes active. We couldn't confirm any of them through an official source in this run, so we're not listing them here.

If you want codes, check the game's own Roblox page and the developer's official community. Those are the only places where you'll know a code is real. And as always, skip any site that promises free Robux or asks for your login.

## Is it worth playing?

**Yes, if you like relaxed tycoons and want something seasonal.** It's easy to pick up, it doesn't punish you, and the theme is a nice break from horror obbies and brainrot games. It's a good game to have on while you chat with friends.

**Maybe skip it if you want depth.** From everything we read, it's a classic earn-and-upgrade loop. If tycoons bore you after an hour, this one probably will too.

**Timing tip:** the game is busiest around its live events. If you want full servers and something happening, play during the next event. To play outside events, expect quieter lots.
