A month ago, **Anime Crusaders** wasn't even playable. On Oct 27 the game was listed as being in maintenance, with zero players online (according to a snapshot on Timesaver). Today it's back under a new name, **Anime Crusaders X**, and it's one of the ten most-played games on Roblox. The tracker Endgametools counted about **93,600 players** in it on Nov 24 at 00:46 UTC, putting it at No. 10 on the whole platform, just ahead of Jujutsu Shenanigans.

That's a big swing for a game that is a year and a half old. Here's what happened, what the relaunch and the anniversary update add, and whether you should give it a try.

## What is Anime Crusaders X?

It's a **tower defense game with anime-style units**. You summon heroes with gems, place them along a path and stop waves of enemies before they get through. Between matches you level up your units and improve them with upgrade items like trait rerolls and stat cubes. If you've played other anime tower defense games on Roblox, you already know the loop: summon, build a team, clear harder stages, summon again.

The game comes from the studio **32x** and was first created on **May 7, 2025** (Rolimons). So "X" isn't a brand-new game. It's the same game page, with the same visit history, relaunched under a new name.

## The comeback, in numbers

The player count has climbed fast since the relaunch. Different trackers checked at different times, so here they are one by one:

- **Nov 16:** about 32,000 players online, according to the fan wiki Anime Crusaders Wiki, which dates the relaunch to around Nov 15–16.
- **Nov 20, 08:10 UTC:** 45,632 players (Timesaver, using Roblox's own data).
- **Nov 22, 07:15 UTC:** 71,193 players (BLOXG).
- **Nov 24, 00:46 UTC:** about 93,600 players and No. 10 overall (Endgametools).

Livecounts, which reads Roblox's "top playing now" list, showed about **103,500 players** at its latest update (13:13 UTC, seen on Nov 24), and ranked it No. 9. Rolimons lists an all-time peak of about **106,000** players, but without a date.

In other words: roughly **triple** the players in about a week. Players also seem to like what they found. BLOXG showed a **98% like rating** on Nov 22, from around 380,000 votes.

## Why did it go down, and what changed?

This is the part nobody has fully explained. None of the sources we read say *why* the original game went into maintenance, and we couldn't find an official statement from 32x about it. Some of the new codes the community found refer to a "shutdown", so the developers clearly know players noticed.

What the fan wikis do agree on:

- The game was **renamed to Anime Crusaders X** and relaunched in mid-November, on the same game page.
- It came back with **new units**, a reworked tier list and a **fresh set of codes**.
- Whether your old progress carried over is **not confirmed** by any source we found. If you played before October, log in and check your units in the lobby before you spend anything.

## The anniversary update

On top of the relaunch, the game is running a two-part **anniversary update**. The title on Roblox currently carries an "ANNI P2" tag, which means Part 2 is live.

According to the fan site CrusadersX Wiki (updated Nov 23), **Part 1** reworked how damage against flying enemies works, added a new Hero slot and a four-player co-op zone. **Part 2** reportedly adds a **Tournament of Power** mode, a **Clan Hideout**, collectible **Anniversary Stars** and more new units. The site dates the Part 2 game update to **Nov 23**, while a second fan wiki points to Nov 22, so treat the exact day as approximate.

One honest warning: these details come from fan wikis, not official patch notes. The developer's own patch archive hasn't been updated for these versions yet, according to the same site. The game's own update log in the lobby is the best place to check what's really in.

## A few tips if you're starting now

- **Join the 32x group and favorite the game first.** Code-tracking sites say the current codes only work after that, and two of them are locked until you reach level 10 or level 20.
- **Get codes from the game itself or 32x's own channels.** We haven't listed codes here because we couldn't confirm them from an official source in this check. Never type your password into any "code" or "free gems" site.
- **Don't spread your gems thin.** In any summon-based tower defense game, a few strong, upgraded units beat a big box of weak ones. Pick a small core team and level that up first.
- **Check the co-op modes.** If you have friends playing, the four-player zone from Part 1 is a nice way to clear harder content together.

## Is it worth playing?

If you like anime tower defense, **yes, this is a good moment to jump in**. The game is at its busiest, the relaunch means lots of players are starting fresh alongside you, and the like ratio is very high. The big unknowns are how long the hype lasts and how much grinding the later stages need.

If tower defense isn't your thing, Anime Crusaders X won't change your mind. It's a solid, popular version of a genre Roblox already has plenty of, not a reinvention. But as comeback stories go, going from zero players to the top 10 in under a month is hard to beat.
