Three months ago, Steal An Egg didn't exist. Today it is the most-played game on Roblox, and it isn't close. In snapshots from public player trackers this week, it had somewhere between about 745,000 and 1.1 million people playing at the same time. Brookhaven RP, Adopt Me! and Blox Fruits together didn't reach that.

So what is it, how did it get this big, and should you play it?

## What you actually do

The full name says most of it: *Steal An Egg and Collect Rare Pets*. You grab eggs, hatch them into pets, and those pets earn money for you. Rarer pets earn more. You spend the money on upgrades and better zones, which run from Forest and Desert all the way to Cosmic and Cherry Blossom.

The twist is in the first word. Other players can steal from your base, and you can steal from theirs. That means speed matters, so you train it up to grab eggs and get away before anyone catches you.

If that sounds familiar, it should. It's the same "build up, steal, defend" loop that made Steal a Brainrot the biggest game of 2025, applied to pets and eggs instead of meme characters.

## How it got so big, so fast

The game launched in July 2026. By September 27, Roblox's own Top Playing Now chart showed it with roughly 1.88 million concurrent players, about four times as many as Brookhaven RP in second place. It has cooled since then, but it is still holding the top spot by a wide margin.

A few things work in its favor:

- **The loop is instantly readable.** Within a minute you understand the goal: get better pets, protect them, take other people's.
- **Stealing creates stories.** Losing your best egg to a stranger, or pulling off a getaway, is exactly the kind of moment people clip and share.
- **It updates constantly.** Right now there's a Halloween Dimension with bosses and secret eggs, and it's only part one of two.

## The treadmill controversy

Steal An Egg's rise wasn't smooth. On August 24, Roblox briefly took the game offline. It was back about three and a half hours later, and within an hour of returning it had over 480,000 players again.

Roblox never gave an official public reason. The most widely reported issue was the treadmill: to gain speed, players could stand on a treadmill that played a scrolling feed of short videos, many of them AI-generated. That feature was removed when the game came back.

A few days later, Roblox announced new rules: games that reward players for watching a continuous feed of content "without natural stopping points" are restricted for younger accounts unless they change those mechanics. Steal An Egg is the reason that rule exists.

## Is the brainrot wave over?

Not really. It's changing shape. Steal a Brainrot is still in the top five, but far below the record of more than 25 million concurrent players it hit in October 2025. Steal An Egg took its formula and swapped the memes for pets. Copycats like Break and Steal an Egg are now climbing too.

The "steal" part is what's sticking around. The meme characters were only the costume.

## Is it worth playing?

If you like collecting and don't mind a bit of chaos, yes. It's free, quick to understand, and more fun with friends who can help you guard your base. If losing your progress to a random thief would ruin your day, it might frustrate you. Just like the original, it's built on that tension.

One tip: ignore anyone in chat or on YouTube offering "free pets" or scripts. Those are the fastest way to lose your account.
