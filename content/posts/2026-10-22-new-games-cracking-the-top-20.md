Most of Roblox's top 20 is made of veterans. Murder Mystery 2 is from 2014. Adopt Me! is from 2017. Blox Fruits is from 2019. That makes it worth paying attention when something brand new pushes its way in.

On the most-played list from October 21, three games from late summer 2026 did exactly that. All three also have some of the best like ratios on the whole list.

## Anime Dice

**Created:** August 15, 2026. **Playing at once:** roughly 70,000–80,000 in snapshots this week. **Like ratio:** 99%.

Anime Dice is a roll-and-collect game. You roll dice to pull anime characters, and better characters raise your Luck, which makes the next rolls better. You can upgrade your dice and reroll a character's Traits to squeeze out more power.

A 99% like ratio is rare for any game, let alone one with tens of thousands of players. That usually means the game feels generous: rolls come fast and progress is easy to see. Its first Halloween event just wrapped up, so watch for part two.

**Try it if:** you like gacha-style collecting and watching numbers go up.

## Break and Steal an Egg

**Created:** September 5, 2026. **Playing at once:** roughly 60,000–90,000 this week. **Like ratio:** 98%.

The name tells you where the idea came from. Here, you train your speed to reach new areas, smash an egg open with a pickaxe, grab the animal inside and run back to your pen without getting caught by guards. Each animal you bring home earns income, which you spend on upgrades.

It's the clearest sign yet that Steal An Egg has started its own sub-genre. The guards and the pickaxe give it more of a heist feel than the original.

**Try it if:** you like [Steal An Egg](/posts/steal-an-egg-biggest-game-on-roblox/) but want a slightly different twist on it.

## Huss Valley

**Created:** August 31, 2026. **Playing at once:** about 50,000 on Oct 21. **Like ratio:** 88%.

Huss Valley is the odd one out, which is exactly why it's interesting. It's not a collecting game at all. It's tag, turned into an arena game. One player starts as the Chaser, everyone else tries to get across the map, and every Runner who gets caught joins the chasers. The last one standing wins.

Movement is built around momentum, and you can only dash sideways, so good players feel noticeably different from new ones. Between rounds you spend coins and gems on abilities and items like Banana Peels and Adrenaline. A Halloween event is running until November 7.

**Try it if:** you want something short, competitive and loud to play with friends.

## What this tells us

Two of the three new hits are variations on games that are already huge: roll-to-collect and steal-to-collect. That's how Roblox trends usually go. One big hit appears, and within weeks the ideas get remixed.

Huss Valley is the exception: a simple playground game with good movement. That's worth watching, because it shows there's still room near the top for something that isn't another simulator.
