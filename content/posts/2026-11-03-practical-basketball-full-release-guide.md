Halloween is over, most spooky events are winding down, and one of the biggest climbers on Roblox right now is... a basketball game. **Practical Basketball** left its long testing phase and went into full release on Oct 30, and within days it was sitting at No. 7 on Endgametools' most-played list with about 100,000 players (snapshot Nov 3, 02:34 UTC). It was also No. 11 on Roblox's own trending chart at that moment. If you've been seeing clips of green releases and posterizing dunks all weekend, this is why.

Here's what changed with the release, how the new ranked mode works, and how to get through your first games without bricking every shot.

## What is Practical Basketball?

It's a skill-based basketball game from the studio Practical's Games. The big idea: no arcade magic. Shooting comes down to timing your release, passing depends on reading the floor, and finishing at the rim depends on spacing and momentum. Guides written during testing described it as having no stat grinding and no assisted shooting, so the better player wins, not the one with more upgrades.

That approach has clearly landed. On Rolimons (checked Nov 3), the game shows about 109 million visits, around 146,000 favorites and a like rating of about 93%. Its all-time peak, 147,175 players at once, was recorded about three days before that check, so right around launch weekend.

Before this, the game spent a long time in on-and-off testing. A fan-run status site lists a third stress test that ended Aug 13, 2026, and players often found the servers closed between test windows. That waiting is a big part of why the full release hit so hard.

## What the full release added

According to the official release event (listed on AllThingsHow on Oct 25, start time Oct 30 at 6 PM ET), launch brought:

- **Ranked Rec** matchmaking, plus **Blacktop** queues from 1v1 up to 5v5
- A **season pass** and a progression system (reputation, build advancement, rewards)
- New jump shots, dribble moves and dunks, plus reworked shot meters
- **Gym customization**, mascots and a codes system
- New cosmetics like shoes, clothing, hairstyles and even a skateboard
- Performance and mobile improvements

RBLX News also reports a limited-time Halloween event at launch and stronger anti-cheat for private runs and leagues.

## How ranked works

Ranked happens in the Rec Center. RBLX News (updated Nov 3) describes it like this: you play a **five-game placement series**, then get sorted into competitive tiers running from **Amateur up to Diamond**.

Our advice: don't jump into placements on day one. Those first five games decide where you start, and if you're still learning the shot timing, you'll place lower than you need to. Get comfortable in Blacktop first, then queue ranked.

## Controls to learn first

One warning: the most complete control lists we found were written during testing (the AllThingsHow guide is from Aug 1, 2026), and that guide itself says inputs can change between builds. Check the in-game prompts if something doesn't match. On PC, the basics were:

- **Shoot:** hold **E** and release at the top of your jump. Tap E for a pump fake.
- **Dunk:** **Space** for a standing dunk, **Shift + Space** for a driving dunk.
- **Pass:** **1–4** to pass to a teammate. **Shift + number** is a chest pass, double-tap is a bounce pass.
- **Alley-oop:** **F** while driving for a self-oop, **T + number** to throw one to a teammate.
- **Dribble moves:** **Z, X, C and V** (V is the step-back).
- **Sprint:** **Shift**.

Controller and mobile both work, but we couldn't find a reliable, up-to-date full button map for either. The game shows on-screen prompts, and the developers keep the current layout in their community server.

## Six tips for your first games

1. **Do the tutorials.** There are six "Learn Practical Basketball" lessons, and they pay out Coins. Easy reward, and you'll actually learn the shot meter.
2. **Fix your release before anything else.** Every shot uses a timing meter. Shoot around with no defense and release at the same point every time. The game tells you when you hit a "Perfect Release" and when a defender contested you, so use that feedback.
3. **Take the easy shot.** Open, stationary jumpers and layups beat fancy step-backs while you're learning. Get one spot on the floor down before you expand your range.
4. **Don't hold sprint all game.** Stamina drains fast. Sprint in short bursts, then slow down and read the defense.
5. **Pass early.** When a second defender comes at you, the open teammate is the play. Practice the simple 1–4 passes before the flashy ones, because sloppy passes fly out of bounds.
6. **Save dunks for open lanes.** If the paint is crowded, the dunk usually fails. Wait for the help defender to commit, then go.

## Is it worth playing?

If you like sports games, yes. Practical Basketball is one of the few Roblox games where getting better feels like *your* doing, not your inventory's. The flip side: it has a real learning curve. Your first few games may be rough, and ranked will feel sweaty fast.

Expect the numbers to bounce around, too. Trackers already disagree: Rolimons showed about 79,000 players and RBLX News about 87,000 on Nov 3, while the game didn't appear in Livecounts' top 25 at 11:03 UTC that day. Launch-week hype always cools a bit. Whether it sticks in the top 10 will depend on how quickly the season pass and ranked keep people coming back. It's at No. 7 on [this week's chart](/chart/), and we'll keep watching.
