Look at the most-played list on Roblox right now and you'll see little pumpkins in front of half the game names. October is the busiest event month of the year, and this year Roblox has its own official Halloween event on top of it: **The Hollow**, which starts today, October 22.

Here's what is confirmed, what is still a guess, and which big games are worth jumping into before their events end.

## What is The Hollow?

The Hollow is Roblox's platform-wide Halloween event for 2026. Roblox teased it in a short trailer on its official X account on October 10. On October 17, it named the nine games taking part:

- DOORS
- Piggy
- 99 Nights in the Forest
- Dandy's World
- The Mimic
- WARDEN
- Demonology
- Animal Hospital
- Scary Shawarma Kiosk: the ANOMALY

That is basically a greatest-hits list of Roblox horror. Some are huge (99 Nights in the Forest regularly has well over 100,000 people playing at once), others are cult favorites.

## How it probably works

Roblox has not explained the rules in detail yet, so treat this part as an educated guess. Recent Roblox events have worked the same way: you hop between the participating games, finish a quest in each one, collect an event currency, and trade it for free avatar items. Roblox's "nine names, nine nights" teaser has people expecting roughly one game per night, but that hasn't been confirmed.

The end date isn't official either. Different outlets mention November 5, 8 or 9, so plan for about two to three weeks.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Scam warning:</strong> There are no codes for The Hollow. You get items by playing the participating games. Any site that promises codes, "free items" or early access in exchange for your login is a scam.</p></div>

## Big games with their own Halloween events

Separate from The Hollow, a lot of top games are running their own Halloween updates. These are the ones from the current most-played list, with end dates (UTC) from each game's event page:

- **Steal An Egg:** a Halloween Dimension with bosses and secret eggs, until Oct 24. It's billed as part 1 of 2, so expect a second wave.
- **Adopt Me!:** Halloween week 4 starts Oct 23 and runs to Oct 30.
- **Brookhaven RP:** trick-or-treating arrives Oct 23 and stays until Nov 6.
- **RIVALS:** RIVALSWEEN runs until Nov 7.
- **Dress To Impress:** a big Halloween and fall update, until Nov 7.
- **Jujutsu Shenanigans:** its Halloween update was pushed back and now starts Oct 23, running for a month.
- **99 Nights in the Forest:** Trick or Treat, part 1 of its event, until Oct 24.
- **Fisch:** FischFright returns for a third year, until Nov 9.
- **Grow a Garden:** Ghoul Garden, with new plants, pets and a Halloween currency, until Oct 24.
- **BedWars:** a three-part event; part 2 brings a new kit and battle pass from Oct 23.
- **Piggy:** Fright Night season 9 until Nov 1, plus a Frightful Forest map until Nov 8.

A few giants have no Halloween event listed on their pages yet, including Murder Mystery 2 and Blox Fruits.

## Our picks if you only have one evening

1. **99 Nights in the Forest.** It's part of The Hollow *and* has its own Trick or Treat event, so you double up.
2. **Fisch.** FischFright is a fan-favorite, and it runs long enough that you won't feel rushed.
3. **Dress To Impress.** Halloween themes are what this game was made for.

## The verdict

If you like horror games, this is the best week of the year to be on Roblox. Start with whichever Hollow game you already know, and keep an eye on the event page in each game for the official quest list. We'll update this post when Roblox confirms the rewards and the end date.
