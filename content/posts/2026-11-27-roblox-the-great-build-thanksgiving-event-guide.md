Roblox's Thanksgiving event is a giant tower that every player builds together, one block at a time. It's called **The Great Build**, it started on November 25, and it climbed to No. 1 on Roblox's Trending chart by the afternoon of November 26, according to the tracker Trendoday. The catch: the official listing says it runs only until **November 30**, and you can only place one free block per hour. If you want the free avatar items, today is a good day to start.

## What is The Great Build?

It's a single experience published under Roblox's own "Roblox Presents" label, according to Streamscharts, so this isn't a fan event or a random game borrowing the name. In the middle of the map stands one huge tower. Gamezebo describes it as "a Tower of Babel-like structure, composed entirely of small terracotta bricks."

Each block you add carries a short gratitude message and your username, and every block makes the shared tower a little taller. That is the Thanksgiving idea: everyone on Roblox adds a "thank you" to the same building.

Around the tower there are side activities: an obby, a time trial, a scavenger hunt and a rewards platform where you claim your items.

## How many people are playing?

Trendoday's tracker, which logs the "playing" number Roblox reports, showed:

- about **47,600** players on Nov 25 (23:22 UTC), the day it launched
- a peak of about **56,100** on Nov 26 (13:22 UTC)
- about **23,400** on Nov 27 (02:52 UTC), the latest reading

Those numbers are well below giants like Steal An Egg, but this is a short event game, not an everyday hangout. What matters more is the Trending chart: it appeared in all 48 of Trendoday's checks on November 26, with an average rank of 4.1, and it was still No. 1 at the latest check early on November 27.

## How the blocks work

This is the part most players miss at first:

1. You get **one free block every hour**. You can't grind more by playing longer, so check back over the next few days.
2. You need to place **five blocks yourself** to qualify for the rewards. That means at least five separate hours of play spread across the event.
3. On top of that, the **whole community** has to hit block milestones. Gamezebo saw a plaque at spawn counting toward 10,000,000 blocks for the next avatar item.

Because of the hourly limit, the smart move is simple: join, place your block, do an activity, and come back later. Five check-ins before November 30 is all it takes for the block rewards.

## Every free item and how to get it

Here's the reward list as reported by Streamscharts on Nov 26. Gamezebo, writing the same day, also lists the obby and scavenger hunt rewards.

**From placing blocks (five of your own, plus a community goal):**

- **Brickle & Pebble**: place five blocks
- **Patchbrick Cap**: community reaches 1 million blocks
- **Snowcapped Brick Tails**: community reaches 5 million blocks
- **Terracotta Skyserpent**: community reaches 10 million blocks
- **The Ascendant Bloom**: community reaches 20 million blocks

**From the activities:**

- **Skybound Monarch** (wings that let you fly in the experience): finish the obby. It starts on a raised tile near the tower's bottom entrance.
- **Cloudtop Tower** (a hat): finish the scavenger hunt
- **Cloudbreaker Horns**: finish the time trial

**From special guests:**

- **Golden Brick Circlet**: collect a launcher item handed out by a Star Creator in your server
- **Sapphire Brick Circlet**: collect one from a Roblox admin in your server

For the last two, just being in the same server isn't enough, and watching a stream doesn't count, according to Streamscharts. You have to actually pick up the item while a creator or admin is handing it out.

## Tips before you jump in

- **Start the obby first.** It's open now, and the Skybound Monarch wings are the flashiest item on the list, and flying makes getting around the map a lot easier.
- **Set a reminder for your hourly block.** Five blocks need five different hours. Spreading them over Friday and Saturday is safer than leaving it to the last day, since Roblox hasn't listed an exact end time for November 30.
- **Write a real message.** Your block shows your name and your words to everyone who climbs past it. Keep it kind; it's a gratitude tower.
- **Don't trust "free item" links.** All rewards come from inside the official experience. You never need to enter your password on another site or download anything to get them.

## Is it worth playing?

Yes, if you like free avatar items. The Great Build costs nothing, the obby gives you a strong item right away, and the block rewards only ask for a few minutes every hour or so. It's not a game you'll still be playing in December, and it isn't meant to be. But with a 20-million-block goal and only until November 30 to get there, every block from every player counts. Go add yours.
